You may ask {{ office_name }} to delete personal information associated with the WhatsApp staff companion or office-management service.

  1. Email {{ contact_email }} from an address known to the office.
  2. Use the subject WhatsApp Data Deletion Request.
  3. Provide your name and the WhatsApp telephone number concerned. Do not send passwords, OTPs, access tokens, or unrelated confidential documents.
  4. The office may contact you through an existing channel to verify identity and prevent fraudulent deletion requests.

Processing the request

After verification, eligible data will ordinarily be deleted or anonymised within 30 days. Confirmation will be sent when the request is completed.

Information that may be retained

Limited records may be retained where required by law, professional duties, court or client-file obligations, security, fraud prevention, dispute preservation, or legitimate recordkeeping. Data held independently by Meta/WhatsApp or another provider is governed by that provider's own deletion process.
